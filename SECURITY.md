# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a security problem. Report it privately through the contact route of Fjeldstad Consulting: the contact alias listed in the owner's local config and on the company website. If you are an organisation member, you may also write to the owner directly.

Include what you found, where (repository, file, commit or URL), how to reproduce it, and what you think the impact is. You will get an acknowledgement, and the finding is handled under the security gate described in fc-rig before any fix is published.

## Scope

All repositories in the Fjeldstad-Consulting organisation and the services they deploy. Client repositories that have been handed over to the client are out of scope; report to the client.

## What not to do

Do not test against production systems with real customer data, do not attempt denial of service, and do not access data that is not yours. Stop as soon as the problem is demonstrated.
